How to Compress Images Online

  1. Select one or more JPG, PNG or WebP images.

  5. Review the results and download your compressed images.

Why Use Our Image Compressor?

Large images can slow down websites and take up unnecessary storage space. This free image compressor helps reduce image file sizes while letting you control quality and dimensions.

The tool works directly in your browser, so you can compress supported images without sending them to a remote server. Compression results vary depending on the image, format and selected settings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can use the tool for free without creating an account.

Which image formats are supported?

You can select JPG, PNG and WebP images. You can also choose JPG, PNG or WebP as the output format.

Will image quality decrease?

Lossy formats such as JPG and WebP may lose some image detail depending on the selected quality. PNG output is lossless, but changing an image to PNG does not guarantee a smaller file.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This page processes selected images locally in your browser.

Can I compress multiple images at once?

Yes. Select multiple supported images, compress them together, and download the results individually.
