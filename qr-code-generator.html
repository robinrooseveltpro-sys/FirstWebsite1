Why Use Our Free QR Code Generator?

QR codes make it easy to share information without requiring someone to type a long URL or message. They are useful for websites, business cards, restaurant menus, product packaging, Wi-Fi access and promotional materials.

This tool creates static QR codes. The content is encoded directly into the QR image rather than relying on a special redirect service. Your QR code does not expire on its own, although the destination website or other encoded information can change or stop working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. Generate and download QR codes without creating an account.

Do QR codes generated here expire?

These are static QR codes and have no built-in expiration date. They continue to work as long as the encoded information remains useful and accessible.

Can I create a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Enter your network name, security type and password. Compatible phones can scan the code to connect to the network.

Can I customize the QR code?

Yes. Change the foreground color, background color, image size and error correction level.

Is my information uploaded?

No. The QR code generation happens in your browser. Avoid sharing QR codes containing sensitive information publicly.
