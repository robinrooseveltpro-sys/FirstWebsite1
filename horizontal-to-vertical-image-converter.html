What this tool actually does

“Horizontal to vertical” can mean two very different things, and mixing them up is the most common mistake:

  • Rotation swaps the width and height of the image. The result is portrait-shaped, but the subject ends up lying on its side. This is what a “rotate 90°” tool produces.
  • Canvas conversion keeps the subject upright and changes the frame around it — by cropping to a portrait shape, padding with a solid color, or filling the empty area with a blurred copy of the image.

This converter supports both. By default it uses Auto, which inspects your image and picks the mode that usually looks best: a blurred background fill for busy photos, or a solid-color pad when the top and bottom edges of the image are already uniform (a sky, a wall, a studio backdrop).

How to use it

  1. Drop or select a landscape image (PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, or BMP).
  2. Pick a target aspect ratio — 9:16 for Stories/Reels/TikTok, 4:5 for Instagram portrait, or a custom size.
  3. Leave the mode on Auto, or force Blur / Solid / Crop / Rotate.
  4. Choose PNG for lossless quality or JPEG/WebP for smaller files, then click Convert.
  5. Check the preview, then download.

Which mode should I choose?

  • Blur background — best when the image is busy edge to edge and you do not want to lose any part of the frame. The subject stays upright, and the empty top/bottom area is filled with a blurred, enlarged copy of the original.
  • Solid color — best when the image has uniform edges (sky, wall, studio backdrop) or when you want a clean, on-brand color block above and below.
  • Center crop — best when the subject is centered and you are comfortable losing the left and right sides. No added pixels, no letterboxing.
  • Rotate 90° — the literal “horizontal to vertical” operation. The shape changes, but the subject turns sideways. Use it only when that is what you actually want.

Privacy

Everything happens in your browser using the HTML Canvas API. Your image is never uploaded, never sent to a server, and never logged. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool will still work.

Limitations

  • GIF and animated WebP output is not supported — only the first frame is processed. Use a dedicated animated converter if you need to preserve motion.
  • Very large images (above roughly 8 megapixels) may be slow to preview on low-end phones. The download still uses the full resolution.
  • EXIF orientation is applied by the browser when the image is decoded. If your source file has an incorrect orientation flag, the result will inherit that.
  • WebP export depends on browser support; if unavailable, the tool falls back to PNG and tells you.
  • When the source image is smaller than the target preset, the output is upscaled. This is normal for social platform presets but adds no real detail.

Frequently asked questions

Does this upload my image to a server?

No. All processing runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

What is the difference between “Blur background” and “Rotate 90°”?

Blur background keeps the subject upright and fills the empty top and bottom area with a blurred, zoomed copy of the image. Rotate 90° physically turns the whole picture sideways.

Will the output be exactly 9:16?

Yes. The output canvas is sized to the exact ratio you choose (for example 1080×1920 for 9:16). The original is fit inside it according to the mode you selected.

Can I use it for Instagram Reels, TikTok, or YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Choose 9:16 and either Blur background or Solid color, then download the result.

Why does my file size get bigger after converting to PNG?

PNG is lossless and typically produces larger files than JPEG. For social uploads, JPEG or WebP at quality 0.9 usually looks indistinguishable and stays smaller.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page has loaded. All conversion is client-side.
