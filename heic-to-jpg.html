Why Convert HEIC to JPG?

Better compatibility

JPG is supported by many browsers, image editors, apps, and online upload forms. Converting HEIC photos can help when a website does not accept the original format.

Convert iPhone photos

HEIC is commonly associated with photos taken on Apple devices. This tool can convert supported HEIC and HEIF files into JPG images without requiring a desktop application.

Choose your JPG quality

Higher quality generally preserves more image detail and may produce larger files. Lower quality can reduce file size but may make compression artifacts more noticeable.
