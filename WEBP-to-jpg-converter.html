How to Convert WebP to JPG Online

WebP is a modern image format, but some apps, websites, and upload forms work better with JPG images. This free converter lets you turn supported WebP files into JPEG images directly in your browser.

  1. Click Select WebP Images and choose your files.
  2. Adjust JPG quality and choose a background color.
  3. Click Convert to JPG to process your images.
  4. Preview and download each converted JPG file.

Features of Our Free WebP to JPG Converter

Convert multiple WebP images

Select several WebP files and convert them one by one. Download each completed JPG separately.

Control image quality

Choose a quality setting from 40% to 100%. Higher quality generally preserves more visual detail, while lower quality often produces smaller files. The actual size depends on the image and its contents.

Choose a background color

JPG cannot retain transparency. If your WebP image has transparent areas, choose a white or black background before conversion.

Convert images inside your browser

Selected image files are processed on your device rather than uploaded to our server. This requires a browser that can decode WebP images. Very large images may exceed your device's memory.

Understand the limitations

JPG uses lossy compression. Converting an image does not guarantee a smaller file, and some image metadata may not be preserved. Keep your original WebP file if you need the source image unchanged.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this WebP to JPG converter free?

Yes. You can convert supported WebP images for free without creating an account.

2. Can I convert multiple WebP files at once?

Yes. Select multiple images and the converter will process them sequentially. Each converted JPG has its own download button.

3. Does JPG support transparency?

No. JPG does not support transparent pixels. This tool fills transparent areas using the white or black background you select.

4. Will converting WebP to JPG reduce quality?

JPG uses lossy compression, so some image information may be discarded. Choose a higher quality setting when visual quality matters more than file size.

5. Why is my WebP image not converting?

The file may be damaged, invalid, or too large for your device. Try opening the image in a browser first, then convert it again.

6. Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The selected image files are processed locally in your browser and are not sent to our server.
