Free invoice generator for freelancers and small businesses

An invoice is a document that records the products or services provided to a customer and the amount requested for payment. A useful invoice generator lets you create that document without manually calculating every line item or total. This online invoice maker gives freelancers, independent contractors, consultants and small businesses a straightforward way to prepare their own invoice.

Use it as a freelance invoice generator, a basic invoice maker for services, or a starting point for an invoice template. Enter the required details, adjust the prices and calculation settings, and review the invoice preview before using the print dialog.

How to create an invoice online

  1. Enter your business or freelancer name and contact details.
  2. Add the client's name, email and billing address.
  3. Choose an invoice number, issue date, due date and currency.
  4. Add each product or service with its quantity and unit price.
  5. Enter any applicable discount, tax percentage and extra charges.
  6. Review the calculated subtotal and final invoice total.
  7. Enter payment instructions and any relevant invoice notes.
  8. Select Print / Save PDF and choose the PDF option in your browser's print dialog.

How the invoice total is calculated

The calculator uses the quantities, prices, discount rate, tax rate and additional charges you enter. Check the formulas below if you want to understand the result or verify it independently.

Line item amount
Quantity × Unit price

Subtotal
Sum of all line item amounts

Discount amount
Subtotal × Discount percentage ÷ 100

Taxable calculation base used by this tool
Subtotal − Discount + Other charges

Tax amount
Taxable calculation base × Tax percentage ÷ 100

Final invoice total
Subtotal − Discount + Other charges + Tax

This is a general calculation model, not a statement of tax law. Depending on your jurisdiction, the correct tax base, required invoice fields, rounding rules and tax treatment may be different. Confirm local requirements before issuing an invoice.

What should a freelance invoice template include?

The requirements for a valid invoice can vary by country, industry and transaction. As a practical starting point, a freelance invoice template commonly includes:

  • Your business or professional name and contact information.
  • Your client's name and relevant billing information.
  • A unique invoice number for identifying the document.
  • The invoice date and payment due date.
  • A clear description of the work, products or services supplied.
  • The quantity, unit price, subtotal and overall amount due.
  • Any applicable tax, discount or additional charge.
  • Payment instructions and any necessary terms or notes.

Some jurisdictions require additional information, such as tax registration numbers, tax breakdowns, currency disclosures or prescribed invoice wording. This general-purpose invoice maker does not determine whether your document complies with those rules.

Create a PDF invoice from your browser

This tool does not require a third-party PDF service. When you use Print / Save PDF, your browser opens its print interface. Select the PDF destination available in your browser or operating system, then review the page size and margins before saving.

The printable preview is designed for ordinary invoice information and includes your business and client details, itemized charges, invoice dates, tax, discount, total, notes and payment instructions. Review the final printed document before sending it to your customer.

Save and reuse invoice drafts

Drafts are saved in local browser storage on the device you use. They are not automatically synced to another device or browser. You can also export a draft as a JSON file and import that file later. Keep exported drafts secure because they can contain personal, customer or business information. The business logo must be selected again after reloading or importing a draft.

Frequently asked questions

Answers about creating and using an online invoice.

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes. The page provides invoice creation, calculations, draft export and browser-based printing without requiring an account or a paid conversion service.

Can I create an invoice as a freelancer?

Yes. You can enter your professional details, describe your work, add hourly or project-based charges, specify a due date, and include payment instructions. Verify any invoice requirements that apply to your location.

How do I save my invoice as a PDF?

Select Print / Save PDF. In the browser's print dialog, choose the available PDF destination, review the print preview, and save the document.

Can I add tax and a discount to my invoice?

Yes. Enter the applicable percentage discount and tax rate. The tool calculates the resulting amounts using the formulas explained on this page. Confirm local tax rules independently.

Can I change the invoice currency?

Yes. You can select from the currencies listed in the editor. This changes how monetary values are displayed; it does not convert the values using foreign exchange rates.

Can I add my business logo?

Yes. Upload a supported image file of up to 2 MB to display it on the invoice. The logo is kept in the current page session and must be selected again after a reload.

Will my draft be available on another device?

No. Automatic draft storage is local to the browser and device where you created it. Use Export draft and Import draft to transfer editable invoice details.

Does this generator guarantee that my invoice is tax compliant?

No. Invoice and tax requirements vary by jurisdiction and transaction. The generator is a general-purpose document tool. Confirm the required fields, tax calculations and applicable rules before issuing an invoice.

Can I edit the invoice after creating it?

Yes. Change the details in the editor and the preview updates automatically. Your draft is also saved in local browser storage. Export a JSON copy if you need a portable backup.
