Create an Invoice Online in a Few Steps

An invoice records the products or services supplied to a customer and the amount requested in payment. With this free online invoice generator, you can prepare a clear document without having to format a spreadsheet or word-processing template manually.

  1. Enter your business name and contact details.
  2. Add your client's name and billing address.
  3. Enter an invoice number, invoice date, and payment due date.
  4. Add each product or service, including its quantity and unit price.
  5. Enter any applicable tax rate, discount, and shipping charge.
  6. Review the live preview, then print the invoice or save it as a PDF.

Check the details carefully before sending the invoice. The amounts are calculated from the information you enter, so incorrect quantities, prices, dates, or tax rates will affect the final document.

What Should a Professional Invoice Include?

The information needed on an invoice depends on the transaction and the rules that apply to your business. A useful starting point is to include the following details:

  • Your business or seller name and appropriate contact details.
  • The customer's name and relevant billing information.
  • A unique invoice number for recordkeeping.
  • The invoice issue date and payment due date, when applicable.
  • A clear description of each product or service.
  • Quantities, unit prices, subtotal, discounts and charges.
  • Applicable taxes and the total amount payable.
  • Payment instructions, reference numbers or terms when needed.

Depending on your jurisdiction and type of transaction, additional information may be required. Confirm local invoicing, tax and recordkeeping requirements before using a generated invoice as an official tax document.

How This Invoice Generator Calculates the Total

Each line amount is calculated by multiplying the quantity by the unit price. The line amounts are added together to create the subtotal. A discount is then deducted, tax is calculated on the discounted subtotal, and any shipping or other charge entered is added.

The calculation used is:

Subtotal = sum of quantity × unit price for all items

Discounted amount = subtotal − discount

Tax = discounted amount × tax rate ÷ 100

Total due = discounted amount + tax + shipping

This is a general calculation method. Different tax systems may use different tax bases, tax-inclusive prices, multiple tax rates or special rounding rules. Check which calculation applies to your transaction.

Who Can Use an Online Invoice Maker?

A simple invoice maker can help freelancers, consultants, independent contractors, designers, developers, service providers and small businesses prepare itemized invoices. It is also useful when you need a straightforward invoice for an individual project or one-off sale.

Select the appropriate currency for the document, add clear payment terms, and verify all numbers before sharing the finished invoice. Choosing a currency changes the displayed currency formatting; it does not convert amounts using a live exchange rate.

Save or Print an Invoice as a PDF

Once the preview is correct, select Print / Save PDF. Your browser opens its print dialog. On computers that support it, select Save as PDF to save a digital copy, or select a connected printer to print the invoice on paper. Options on mobile devices depend on the browser and operating system.

Review the paper size, margins, and preview before saving. If your invoice contains many line items, check that the table and totals are readable across all pages. Keep a copy of the invoice for your records.

Important Limitations

This page creates an invoice document; it is not a complete accounting or billing platform. It does not collect payment, send invoices to customers, file taxes, verify tax registration, or confirm legal compliance. It also does not obtain live currency exchange rates.

Use the tax, currency and billing fields that apply to your transaction, and seek advice from a qualified professional when you need help with local tax treatment or legal requirements.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I create an invoice online for free?

Enter your business and client details, add line items with quantities and prices, set the dates, and enter any relevant tax or discount. Review the preview and use Print / Save PDF to create a copy.

2. Can I download my invoice as a PDF?

Yes. Select Print / Save PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog when that option is available. You can also print the invoice using a connected printer.

3. Can I add my company logo to an invoice?

Yes. Upload a supported PNG, JPEG or WebP image in the editor. The logo appears in the preview and is included in print output.

4. Can I create invoices in Nepalese rupees or other currencies?

Yes. The currency selector includes NPR, USD, INR, EUR, GBP and several other currencies. Selecting a currency changes the displayed currency format, but does not perform exchange-rate conversion.

5. How are taxes and discounts calculated?

The tool calculates item amounts from quantity and rate, applies the entered discount to the subtotal, calculates tax on the discounted subtotal, and adds shipping or other charges. Check that this method matches the rules for your transaction.

6. Does this invoice generator save my work?

It attempts to save your draft in this browser's local storage. Storage may be unavailable or cleared by your browser, so use Export Draft to download a JSON backup if you need to keep your work.

7. Can I edit the invoice after downloading the PDF?

The PDF is a document copy. To make further changes in this tool, return to the editor and update your fields, or import a JSON backup that you exported earlier. The PDF itself is not an editable form within this tool.

8. Does this tool replace accounting software?

No. It prepares invoice documents and calculates the amounts entered. It does not manage accounts, process payments, file tax returns, or determine whether an invoice meets every local legal requirement.
