What you can contact us about

Here are the kinds of messages that are most useful to us.

Report a problem

If a tool gives the wrong result, freezes, or fails on a specific file, tell us the details and we will look into it.

  • Which tool page you were using
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Your browser and device (for example, Chrome on Android)

Suggestions and feedback

We are still growing the tool collection, so suggestions genuinely shape what gets built next.

  • Ideas for new tools you would find useful
  • Feedback on how an existing tool could be clearer
  • Accessibility or mobile usability problems
  • Corrections to instructions or explanations

Before you get in touch

A couple of quick checks can save you time and help us help you faster.

  • Check the tool's own instructions first. Most pages explain supported formats, size limits, and any known limitations near the tool itself.
  • Try a different browser or refresh the page. Some issues come from an out-of-date cached version of a page.
  • Do not email us your private files. Please never send personal documents, ID scans, or sensitive data. Describe the problem instead — that is enough for us to investigate.
  • Be specific in the subject line. Something like "Image Compressor fails on large PNG" helps us route your message correctly.
About response times: ConvertToToolsOnline is run by a small team, so we cannot promise an instant reply. We do read every message, and we aim to respond to genuine enquiries as quickly as we reasonably can. Thank you for your patience.

A quick privacy note

When you contact us by email or phone, you are sharing your details directly with us through those services. We use the information only to reply to your message and to improve the tools on this website. For full details about how this site handles data, please read our Privacy Policy.
