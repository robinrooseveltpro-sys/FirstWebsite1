What Is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer changes the pixel dimensions of a digital image. You can use this tool to prepare photographs for websites, presentations, social media, online forms, and other digital projects that require particular image dimensions.

For example, a 2400 × 1600 pixel photograph can be resized to 1200 × 800 pixels while preserving its original proportions. Reducing dimensions can reduce file size, although the result depends on the image format and its content.

Understanding Image Formats

JPG: Commonly used for photographs. It supports adjustable lossy compression but does not preserve transparency.

PNG: Suitable for graphics and images requiring lossless encoding and transparency.

WebP: A modern web image format that supports lossy and lossless compression, including transparency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image resizer free?

Yes. You can resize supported images directly in your browser without a paid image-editing application.

Can I resize images on my mobile phone?

Yes. This page is designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers. Very large images may require more device memory.

What does maintaining aspect ratio mean?

Aspect ratio describes the relationship between an image's width and height. Maintaining it helps prevent the image from looking stretched or squashed.

Will resizing reduce image quality?

Reducing an image's dimensions removes pixel detail. Enlarging it again may make it look less sharp. JPG and WebP quality settings can also affect the final appearance.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No image upload to a server is intentionally performed by this tool. The image is processed locally in your browser. Avoid using untrusted devices for sensitive files.
