Use this free online PDF merger to combine separate documents into one convenient PDF. No account is required.

  1. Select two or more PDF files from your device.

  4. Download your new PDF when processing finishes.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is this PDF merger free?

Yes. You can combine PDF files for free without signing up.

2. How many PDF files can I merge?

You can select multiple PDFs. The practical limit depends on your device's available memory and the combined document sizes.

3. Are my documents uploaded?

No. This tool processes selected PDF documents in your browser rather than uploading them to our server.

4. Can I change the order before merging?

Yes. Use the up and down arrow buttons beside each file to rearrange the documents before creating the merged PDF.

5. Why did my PDF fail to merge?

The file could be password-protected, damaged, or unsupported. Try opening it in a PDF reader first. Very large files can also exceed the memory available to your browser.
