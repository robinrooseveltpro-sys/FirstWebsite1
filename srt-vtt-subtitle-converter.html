    What does an SRT / VTT subtitle converter do?

    SRT (SubRip) and VTT (WebVTT) are text-based formats that describe subtitle or caption text and when it should appear. SRT commonly uses a comma before milliseconds; WebVTT uses a period and begins with a WEBVTT header.

    How to convert SRT to VTT

    1. Choose an SRT file or paste its content.
    2. Leave input format on Auto-detect or select SRT.
    3. Choose VTT as the output format.
    4. Keep the timing shift at zero unless you need to correct synchronization.
    5. Process the file, review any warnings, and download the VTT file.

    How to fix subtitles that are out of sync

    If captions appear two seconds too early, enter 2 to move them later. If captions appear two seconds too late, enter -2 to move them earlier. Small adjustments such as 0.5 or -0.3 are supported.

    What the validation warnings mean

    • Malformed timing: the timestamp line does not match the supported format.
    • Overlapping cues: two consecutive entries in the input timeline overlap.
    • Out-of-order cues: a later entry starts before the previous entry.
    • Omitted cues: shifting made a cue's end time equal to or earlier than its start time.

    Important format limitations

    SRT-to-VTT conversion is not a complete conversion of every advanced caption feature. When converting VTT to SRT, cue identifiers, cue positioning settings, and VTT-specific metadata cannot be represented in ordinary SRT, so they are not included. When converting VTT to VTT, cue identifiers and inline cue settings are retained where present.

    This tool checks common timestamp and sequence problems, but it does not guarantee compliance with every player, broadcasting, accessibility or streaming specification. It does not create subtitles from audio or translate caption text.

    Does the tool upload my subtitle file?

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I convert SRT to VTT online?

    Load or paste your SRT content, select VTT as the output format, process it, and download the resulting WebVTT subtitle file.

    How do I convert VTT to SRT?

    Select VTT as the input format and SRT as the output format. The converter changes timestamps to SRT style and generates sequential SRT cue numbers.

    How can I delay subtitles by two seconds?

    Enter 2 in the timing-shift field. Positive values move captions later; negative values move them earlier.

    Can I shift subtitles earlier using a negative number?

    Yes. For example, -1.5 shifts each cue 1.5 seconds earlier. Cues that no longer have a valid duration after shifting are omitted and counted in the results.

    Does this tool generate subtitles from a video?

    No. It converts and adjusts existing subtitle text files. It does not perform speech recognition, audio transcription or automatic translation.

    Will VTT styling and positioning be preserved in SRT?

    Not completely. SRT has less structured support for cue settings than WebVTT. VTT identifiers and positioning settings are not carried into SRT output.

    Can I use this for a large subtitle file?

    It can process ordinary text subtitle files in the browser, subject to your device's memory and performance. Very large or unusually structured files may need separate inspection.
