How to calculate working days

This working days calculator counts the dates in your chosen period and removes the non-working days you specify. You can adapt it to different schedules instead of relying on one fixed definition of a business day.

1

Choose your dates

Select the start and end dates. Decide whether each boundary should be included in the count.

2

Set weekends and holidays

Select your non-working weekdays and enter any additional holiday dates. The calculator does not assume a country's official holiday calendar.

3

Calculate and export

Get the working-day total, excluded-day breakdown, estimated hours, and a CSV summary that you can save or open in a spreadsheet.

How is the result calculated?

Every counted date is classified as a weekend, a holiday on an otherwise working day, or a working day. A holiday that falls on a selected weekend is counted only as a weekend.

Working days = Calendar days counted − Weekend days excluded − Holidays excluded

For example, Monday through Friday are working days in a typical five-day schedule. If a date range contains 10 weekdays and one supplied holiday falls on a weekday, the result is 9 working days. The actual total depends on the dates and settings you choose.

Frequently asked questions

What is a working days calculator?

It counts working days within a date range after excluding selected weekends and any holiday dates you supply. It can be useful for project planning, leave estimates, schedules, and business-day calculations.

Are the start and end dates included?

Both dates are included by default. You can turn either option off to exclude that boundary. If a boundary falls on a selected weekend or holiday, it is not counted as a working day.

Does this calculator automatically know public holidays?

No. Public holidays depend on country, region, organization, and sometimes local policy. Enter the applicable dates yourself. This avoids presenting an unverified holiday calendar as official.

Can I calculate working days for a six-day week?

Yes. Choose only the weekday or weekdays that are non-working days. For example, select Sunday as the only weekend day if Saturday is a normal working day in your schedule.

How are holidays that fall on weekends handled?

If a supplied holiday falls on a selected non-working weekday, it is included in the weekend exclusion total and is not deducted again as a separate holiday.

Are working hours the same as working days?

No. Estimated working hours equal the calculated working days multiplied by the daily hours you enter. This is an estimate; it does not account for varying shifts, part-time days, or breaks unless you adjust your assumptions.

Is my information uploaded?

The date calculations and CSV generation run locally in your browser. This page's tool code does not send your dates or holiday list to a calculation server. Website hosting, analytics, or advertising services added separately may have their own data practices.
