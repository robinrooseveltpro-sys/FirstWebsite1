Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This is not a template copied from a legal generator. It is a short description of what actually happens when you use this site, written by the person who runs it. If something here is unclear, email me and I will explain it in plain terms.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no logins, and no newsletters.
  • Some tools run entirely in your browser. Your file never leaves your device on those.
  • Other tools send your file to a server to be processed. Each tool page says which one it is.
  • Server-side files are deleted after processing. They are not logged or shared.
  • This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no tracking scripts.
  • No third-party advertising runs on this site today. If that changes, this page will say so first.

1. Who is responsible for your data

ConvertToToolsOnline is run by Rabin Pandey, an individual based in Kathmandu, Nepal. There is no company, no legal entity, and no team. I am the only person who has access to anything described on this page. You can reach me at rabinpandey787@gmail.com or through the contact page.

2. What information is collected

Here is the complete list of information this site receives about you, and under what conditions.

Information you send deliberately

  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, I receive whatever you type into it, along with the email address you provide so I can reply. It is stored in a normal email inbox and nowhere else.
  • Direct email. If you email me directly, I receive your email address and the message. Same as above.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. The web server that runs this site keeps standard access logs, the same as almost every web server on the internet. That includes IP address, the page requested, the time of the request, the browser's user-agent string, and the referring URL. These logs exist to catch abuse and to diagnose problems. They are not analysed for marketing and are not sold.

Information this site does not collect

  • No account names or passwords, because there are no accounts.
  • No newsletter subscription data, because there is no newsletter.
  • No payment information, because nothing on this site costs money.
  • No cross-site tracking, no behavioural profiles, no advertising IDs.

3. What happens to files you upload

This depends entirely on which tool you use. There are two patterns, and each tool page states which one applies to it.

Browser-only tools

Some tools process your file entirely inside your browser using JavaScript. The file is read into memory, transformed, and offered back to you as a download. Nothing is transmitted to a server. If you turn off your internet connection after the page loads, the tool still works. You can verify this yourself with your browser's developer tools.

Server-side tools

Other tools are not possible to run in a browser alone, usually because they need libraries or resources that are too large. On these tools, your file is uploaded to the server, processed there, and the result is returned to you. The upload is deleted as soon as processing finishes, or within a short automatic cleanup window if the process crashes. Files are not inspected, not retained, and not shared with anyone.

If a tool page does not clearly state which pattern it uses, assume it is server-side and act accordingly. If a file is sensitive (an ID scan, a contract, a medical document), check the note on the tool's page before uploading, and prefer a browser-only tool when one exists.

4. Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies of its own. There is no login session to remember, no preference to store, and no analytics cookie to identify you across visits.

The web server may set technically necessary cookies for load balancing or to protect against abuse. Those are session cookies, they expire when you close the browser, and they contain no personal information.

There are no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting scripts, and no third-party analytics running on this site. If that ever changes, this section will be updated before the change goes live.

5. Third parties involved

Running a website unavoidably involves a few service providers. Each one sees only what it needs to do its job.

  • Hosting provider. A VPS hosted in the European Union. It runs the web server and stores the server logs described above. Its own privacy policy applies to that infrastructure.
  • Email provider. Google, through Gmail, handles the inbox that contact form messages and direct emails arrive in. Google's own privacy terms apply to that email traffic.
  • DNS and email lookup APIs. The DNS Lookup, DNSSEC Checker, and Email Authentication Troubleshooter tools send a domain name (and only a domain name) to a public DNS resolver to get answers. They do not send your IP address or anything about you, only the domain you typed in.

There are no other third-party services. No CDN, no fonts hosted elsewhere, no icon library loaded from a third-party domain, no session recording, no error tracker.

6. Advertising (current and future)

Right now, no advertising runs on this site. There are no ad slots, no ad scripts, and no advertising cookies.

At some point in the future, this may change. If small display ads are added (most likely through Google AdSense), the following applies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, would use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this site or to other sites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies would enable it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You would be able to opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.
  • You would also be able to opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

This section exists in advance so that this policy is accurate the moment ads are introduced, without needing a rewrite. If ads are ever added, this section will be updated to say "does" instead of "would" on the day they go live, before anything else changes on the site.

7. Your rights

Because this site collects very little, most privacy rights are moot in practice. But they still apply, and you can exercise any of them by emailing me.

  • Access. You can ask what information I have about you. In almost every case this will be "your email address and the message you sent," and nothing more.
  • Deletion. You can ask me to delete any email or contact form submission you sent. I will delete it, and confirm when it is done.
  • Correction. If you sent a message with a typo or outdated information, I will update my copy.
  • Objection. You can object to any processing of your data. Since so little is done, the practical answer is usually "I will stop replying to you and delete the correspondence," which I will do on request.
  • Complaint. If you believe I am mishandling your data, you can complain to me first, and if that does not resolve it, to the data protection authority in your country. Nepal does not have a dedicated DPA yet, so if you are in the EU or UK, the relevant authority is usually the one in your own member state.

8. How long data is kept

  • Contact form messages and emails. Kept until they are no longer needed. For a resolved bug report, that might be months. For a request with no follow-up, it might be deleted within a few weeks. Nothing is kept indefinitely without reason.
  • Server logs. Rotated on a rolling basis, typically every 14 to 30 days. After rotation, older logs are deleted.
  • Uploaded files. Deleted as soon as processing finishes, or within the automatic cleanup window if processing fails. Nothing is retained.

9. Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. There are no accounts, no chat features, no user profiles, and no way for a child to submit personal information beyond the contact form. If you are a parent and believe your child has sent a message through the contact form, email me and I will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated when something material changes. For example, if advertising is added, or if a third-party service is introduced, or if the way files are processed changes. The date at the top of the page is the date of the most recent revision.

Substantive changes will be announced on the homepage FAQ for at least a couple of weeks before they take effect, so you are not caught off guard.
