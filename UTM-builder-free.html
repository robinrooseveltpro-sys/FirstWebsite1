What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are values appended to a destination URL. Analytics tools can use them to identify the source, medium, and campaign associated with incoming visits. Properly named campaign links help make reports easier to organize and interpret.

What do source, medium, and campaign mean?

  • Source: identifies where the traffic came from, such as Google or a newsletter.
  • Medium: describes the channel, such as CPC advertising, email, or social.
  • Campaign: identifies the particular promotion or marketing initiative.
  • Content: can distinguish specific creative versions or links.
  • Term: can identify a paid keyword or another agreed campaign label.

Why use bulk UTM generation?

Agencies, affiliate marketers, and teams running many ads may need to tag multiple destinations or link variations. Generating links from a CSV can reduce repetitive manual work and make campaign naming more consistent. Always inspect the exported file before importing it into another platform.

Frequently asked questions

Is this UTM builder free?

Yes. The tool generates and exports tracking links without a subscription or account.

Does the UTM builder send my links to a server?

No. This page's URL processing and CSV functions operate locally in the browser. It does not send the data you enter to a server.

Can I generate hundreds of URLs at once?

The bulk builder accepts up to 1,000 data rows per run. Large CSV files may take more time or memory depending on your device and browser.

Do UTM parameters guarantee accurate analytics tracking?

No. Correct parameters help label campaign traffic, but measurement depends on your analytics configuration, consent choices, redirects, browser behavior, and other implementation details.

Should I use lowercase values?

Use one consistent convention. Google Analytics campaign values may be case-sensitive, so values such as Google and google can be treated differently. This tool optionally converts UTM values to lowercase.

Will UTM tags work on every type of link?

This builder is designed for regular HTTP and HTTPS web URLs. Mobile app campaigns may need the platform-specific campaign URL builder and tracking configuration recommended by the platform.
