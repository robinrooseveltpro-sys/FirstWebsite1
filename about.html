About this site

One developer. Free tools. No investors to please.

I'm Rabin. ConvertToToolsOnline is what I work on in the evenings and on weekends, a slow-growing collection of small browser tools that solve the exact problems I kept hitting myself. It is not a startup, and there is no team behind it. Just a laptop, a cheap VPS, and a stubborn refusal to put a login wall in front of a file converter.

  • Based in Nepal
  • Solo developer

  • Reader funded

Why this exists

It started with a PDF that would not shrink.

A few years ago I was applying for something that only accepted PDF uploads under 2 MB. The document was 6 MB, mostly because of a scanned signature someone had inserted as a photo. Every "free" online compressor I tried wanted either an account, a credit card, or a watermark on the output. One of them sent me an email three days later asking why I hadn't upgraded. That was the moment the idea clicked. The tools are not hard. The business models around them are just hostile.

ConvertToToolsOnline is my attempt at the opposite. Every tool opens the moment the page loads. Nothing asks for your email. If a file has to be processed on a server, the page says so before you upload. If it runs in your browser, it runs in your browser. That is the entire contract.

Over time, the site grew from one tool to the list you see on the homepage. Some of them, like the DNS lookup and the HAR file viewer, exist because I needed them while debugging something at work. Others, like the image compressor and the PDF tools, are there because people kept searching for them and I figured I could build a cleaner version.

How it actually runs

The rules I try to keep, even when nobody is watching.

These are not marketing promises. They are the small decisions that keep the site honest, and the ones that keep it out of trouble with search engines and the people who actually use it.

01

No account, ever

You should not have to trade an email address for a file converter. If a tool ever needs a login, it will not be listed here.

02

Say where the file goes

Browser-based tools are labelled as such. Anything that uploads a file to a server says so on its own page, in plain English, before you click.

03

No dark-pattern ads

If and when ads appear, they will sit in clearly labelled slots. No fake download buttons, no "continue" prompts dressed up as part of the tool. If you ever see one that does, tell me and I will fix it.

04

No selling of data

Files you upload are not kept after processing and not sold to anyone. The privacy page explains exactly what is stored and for how long.

05

No SEO spam pages

If a tool is listed, it is a real tool. No "coming soon" placeholders, no 500-word filler pages written to rank for a keyword with nothing behind them.

06

Breakages get fixed

Reports get read in batches, sometimes a week late. But they get read. Every broken tool that has been reported here has been fixed eventually.

Transparency

How the site pays for itself.

Hosting, the domain renewal, and the occasional paid API for the DNS and email tools cost money. Nothing here comes from a VC, and I have no plans to add a subscription. Right now the bill is covered by readers. There is a support page, and some people send a coffee's worth of money after a tool saves them an afternoon. It adds up more than you would expect, and it is genuinely appreciated.

At some point, once the site is big enough to qualify, I will add small display ads from Google AdSense. They will sit in labelled slots, load only after you consent, and never disguise themselves as buttons or download links. I would rather the site stay free with a couple of small ads than put a login wall in front of a file converter.

I may also take the occasional sponsored placement from a developer tool I actually use. If that happens, it will be clearly marked and priced at a flat rate. Anything that does not belong on a utility site gets turned down.

That is the whole plan. No affiliate links hidden inside the tools, no data brokers, no "premium" tier. If any of this changes, this page will change first, before anything else on the site.
