How to calculate freelance project profit

A project payment is not the same as the money you keep. Fees, project costs and unpaid working hours can make a seemingly profitable job earn less than expected. Use this calculator to inspect those costs before setting your next price.

Account for fees

Include applicable marketplace and payment-processing fees. Check your actual provider terms because fee structures can differ.

Measure your real hourly rate

Divide the estimated amount retained by your actual working hours to see what the project earned per hour.

Price future projects

Use the target-based quote to estimate the price required to cover the same cost assumptions and meet your desired hourly earnings.

Calculation formulas and assumptions

The calculator treats both percentage-based fees as percentages of the full project payment. Additional expenses are subtracted afterward. This is a model; use actual provider fee rules if your payment arrangement works differently.

Total fees = Project payment × (Platform fee % + Processing fee %) / 100 Profit before reserve = Project payment − Total fees − Project expenses Tax reserve = max(0, Profit before reserve) × Tax reserve % Estimated retained amount = Profit before reserve − Tax reserve Effective hourly earnings = Estimated retained amount ÷ Actual hours worked

The target quote is calculated by increasing the project payment enough to cover the entered expenses, modeled percentage fees, and the desired earnings after the tax reserve. It assumes the entered fee percentages remain constant at the higher quote.

The tax reserve is a planning assumption, not a calculation of actual tax liability. This tool does not account for every possible cost, tax rule, refund, currency conversion, or platform-specific fee arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

What is a freelance project profit calculator?

It estimates the earnings remaining from a freelance project after the fees and costs you enter. It can also calculate an effective hourly rate based on the hours you actually worked.

How do I calculate my effective hourly earnings?

Subtract your modeled fees, project expenses and optional tax reserve from the project payment. Divide the remaining amount by the actual hours worked. A negative result indicates that the modeled costs exceed the project payment.

How does the recommended project quote work?

The calculator works backward from your target earnings per actual hour. It estimates the gross project payment required to cover your entered expenses and the modeled percentage fees while leaving your target amount after the tax reserve.

Does this calculator use current marketplace fees?

No. You enter the fee percentages yourself. Marketplace charges, withdrawal fees and payment-processing rates may change or differ by account, service, payment method and contract. Check your actual fee schedule before relying on an estimate.

Does the tax reserve equal my actual tax?

No. It is a user-selected planning percentage applied to positive modeled profit after fees and expenses. Actual tax treatment depends on your circumstances and applicable rules. Consult an appropriately qualified tax professional when needed.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Select one of the available display currencies. This only changes the currency formatting; it does not convert exchange rates or turn amounts entered in one currency into another. Use a single currency consistently for one calculation.

Is my project information uploaded?

The calculations, report copying and CSV creation run in your browser. This page's tool code does not transmit the project figures to a calculation server. Hosting, analytics or advertising services configured separately may have their own data practices.
